## Evidence policy

- Official rules override marketing pages.
- Official Arm documentation and upstream repositories control technical facts.
- Community posts can generate hypotheses but cannot prove requirements or
  benchmark claims.
- Web pages can change; important rule decisions must retain access dates and
  contradictions.
